The Entity

An interactive altar-like installation I worked on as a part of a university group project. It aims to induce the feeling of sublime, as the participants face an entity that seemingly possesses more power than they do. The core interaction involved users finding a correct combination of artifacts by placing them on the altar and then pumping the "heart" of the cybernetic idol, thus reviving it.

I developed the main state machine in Chataigne that contained the main execution logic, allowing the installation to "loop" to the beginning without manually resetting it. Chataigne also served as a centralized system for collecting, processing and routing signals collected from an ESP32 controlling artifact RFID scanners and initializing light sensor, as well as another ESP32 governing the pressure sensors and vibration feedback of the "heart".

Outputs of the system included the LED strips, visuals controlled by Touch Designer and a dynamic audio system. I implemented the audio system using Pure Data with sounds designed in Reaper and actuated through user inputs, routed from Chataigne through OSC commands. When recording sounds, along traditional microphones, I utilized an electromagnetic microphone (Ether V2).

Granular Synthesis controlled by L-Systems

vishnya · lgsnth

Above are the results of the experiment, where I attempted to use L-systems to modify an audio signal. The chosen sound manipulations are based on the theory of granular synthesis. I defined a grammar with variable "F" representing a forwards playback of a grain and "B" - backwards playback. Grammar constants "+" and "-" are used to control carrier position the input sound, "D" and "d" were used to adjust duration of the grain. I used branching ("[" and "]") to overlap recursive structures, with depth of the branch controlling the pitch of the grains.

A celestial flower

Some visuals I made in Touch Designer. The repeating shape is a simple f(x,y)=x^2 + y^2 paraboloid, with its domain restricted to [-1,1] for x and y values. It looked like a tulip flower, so I decided to make a rose out of it. I instanced the shape with different scales and added rotation and some noise.

Frequency domain stretching

A small experiment in Python where an audio signal is transformed by uniformly scaling and shifting its frequency axis inside a Short-Time Fourier Transform. Resulting effect is a distorted pitch shift.

A Ruin

vishnya · aruin

"This sound collage explores the feeling of panic, dissociation and pulls the listener into the chaos reigning in the vast expanse of a distressed consciousness. An overloaded conveyor belt of voices and thoughts being stuffed in one's head, each new one shoving the chain of previous ones deeper and deeper, inevitably leading to a collapse, or a rebirth. The work does not include any coherent dialogue, instead it manipulates field recordings of various everyday objects and environments, allowing their unique, imperfect and unpredictable nature to come to light and guide the narrative. They are combined in a lush landscape with a sole purpose of stimulating the imagination, prompting the listener to envision their own story while steering away from comfort and mundanity."
